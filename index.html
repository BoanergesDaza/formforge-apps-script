How we work

  1. You fill in the assessment

    Three steps: your details, your work and your documents. No Google account needed.

  2. We review your case

    We read your university guide and your draft, in strict confidentiality.

  3. We reply by email

    Within 24 to 48 business hours, with the assessment, the timeline and the cost.

  4. We agree on deliveries

    The whole work or chapter by chapter, following the dates your university requires.

  5. You review each part

    If your tutor remarks on something, we help you address it before the next delivery.

  6. You reach your defense

    With the document in your faculty standard, ready for the committee.

Strictly confidential

A thesis takes months of reading, method and writing, and it almost always arrives when you are already working or closing other courses.

That is why Example Academy exists: so your final work keeps moving even when your time falls short.

Everything you send us, your ID, your university guide and your draft, is stored in a private folder under your name. We only use it for your project.

First we give you a clear assessment: what is missing, how long it takes and how much it costs. You decide whether we go ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Online service for students worldwide Reply by email within 24 to 48 business hours Bachelor, diploma, specialization, master and doctorate

How much does it cost?

It depends on the type of work, the number of pages and the deadline. With the assessment we send you a proposal with the exact cost.

What do I need to send?

A photo of your ID, the guide or regulations of your university and your draft or your tutor remarks, if you already have them.

I do not know how many pages it should have

The length is usually in your faculty guide. In the assessment you can enter a range, for example 50-140.

Do you write the thesis for me?

No. We coach and edit: the work, its ideas and its authorship stay yours, as your university requires.

Can you help with the similarity report?

Yes. We help you understand the report and fix citations and references. We do not hide copied text.

Can I get feedback chapter by chapter?

Yes. If your university asks for partial drafts, we agree on a date for each chapter. Mention it in the comments of the assessment.
